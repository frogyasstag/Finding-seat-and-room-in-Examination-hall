Mandatory Examination Rules

Failure to comply with university examination regulations may lead to disqualification.

  • 1. Reporting Window: Candidates must occupy their assigned seats at least 20 minutes before the scheduled start time. Entry closes 15 minutes after commencement.
  • 2. Mandatory Documents: Physical University ID Card and printed Hall Ticket/Admit Card are compulsory for hall entry.
  • 3. Prohibited Electronics: Smartwatches, mobile phones, Bluetooth earbuds, and programmable calculators are strictly banned inside exam halls.
  • 4. Seat Discipline: Occupy only the desk matching your exact Seat Number on SeatSpot. Unauthorized seat swapping is treated as malpractice.
  • 5. Attendance Sheet: Sign the invigilator's attendance sheet and verify your answer booklet serial number before writing.
